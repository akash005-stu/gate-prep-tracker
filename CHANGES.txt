  again from what is left divided by the study days until each deadline, so
  missing a day spreads the work over the days that remain.

  chips On track / Ahead / Speed up / Tight.

- Plan day/week/phase line, schedule and plan rules are unchanged.
- After pushing, hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R). Service worker cache is now v7.

LECTURE TRACKER — now for everyone
==================================

WHAT CHANGED
- No more hardcoded GATE subjects. Anyone can add, edit and remove their own
  subjects: a name, how many lectures, a finish-by date, and how many are
  already done.
- Targets are worked out for you. Every day the app divides what is left by the
  study days remaining until the deadline: "about 1.6 a day", "this week 9 / 11".
  Miss a day and the work is simply spread over the days that are left, so
  nothing piles up (this replaces the old "40 lectures behind" re-balance).
- Each subject shows a small status: On track, Ahead, Speed up, Tight, Done,
  or Past deadline (with a "Set a new deadline" link).
- Log with the + and - buttons on each card. "Log a past day" and a recent
  activity list (with delete) sit underneath.
- Settings: your name, rest days (targets skip them), export your data, delete
  all data, sign out.
- Leaderboard: rank by Overall % or This week, gold/silver/bronze medals,
  active in the last 14 days, opt out any time.
- Light and dark mode follow the phone/computer setting.
- Forgot-password link on the login screen.

YOUR EXISTING DATA
- Mathematics, Applied Engineering Mechanics and Strength of Materials, their
  totals, start date, target date and every logged lecture are converted
  automatically the first time you open the new version.
- Your old timer sessions and settings are kept untouched in the account (under
  "legacy"), they are just not shown.

REMOVED TO KEEP IT MINIMAL
- Flowmodoro focus timer, theme/appearance picker, full weekly plan table,
  import-from-file.
