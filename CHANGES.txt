WHAT'S NEW
==========

1. BACKLOG RE-BALANCE (no more pile-up guilt)
   - When you are more than 40 lectures behind plan, the app re-balances
     automatically after your account has synced.
   - It starts a fresh plan from this week's Monday: the lectures still left are
     spread over the days until your target date (keeping a 3-week cushion
     before it), and new weekday / weekend / weekly (per subject) targets are
     worked out from that.
   - The backlog then reads as zero ("On schedule"). Nothing from the past is
     owed. Past weeks show "Re-planned" instead of "Missed target".
   - A card on the dashboard explains what changed, with an Undo button.
   - If the load would be unrealistic (e.g. near the target date), it is NOT
     applied automatically. The card suggests moving the target date, and a
     "Re-balance anyway" button is offered.
   - Plan settings has two new options: auto re-balance on/off, and the backlog
     limit (default 40). Weekly and daily targets now accept halves (e.g. 2.5).
   - Editing the plan numbers by hand replaces an automatic adjustment.
   - Your original plan is never overwritten.

2. LEADERBOARD TAB
   - Ranks students by % of the syllabus finished. First place gets the gold
     medal (with a spotlight card); 2nd and 3rd get silver and bronze.
   - "Active this week" (opened the app in the last 7 days) or "Everyone".
   - Refreshes every minute while open. Opt out any time with the checkbox.
   - Needs one Firestore rules update: see FIRESTORE-RULES.txt.
